## Purpose

Choose **which** of the approved evidence reaches the CV, before any of it is rewritten.

[[C2. Map JD Requirements to Supporting Evidence]] proposes every honest requirement→evidence link
and the owner approves rows one at a time. Nothing between that and
[[C4. Transform Evidence into CV Bullets]] ever chooses a *set*, so section sizes track how much
evidence was approved rather than what a two-page CV can hold. This step is where that choice is
made: it decides what C4 is even asked to rewrite.

## Status

**Stub.** The step's number and note exist; its procedure does not yet. It is specified in
[[C3 Selects the CV Evidence Set]] and built by that CI.

Until then this note is deliberately **not** wired into `lib/prompts.ts` (`STEP_NOTE`). The step
