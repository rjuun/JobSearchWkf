## Purpose

Choose **which** of the approved evidence reaches the CV, before any of it is rewritten.

[[C2. Map JD Requirements to Supporting Evidence]] proposes every honest requirement→evidence link
and the owner approves the map in one action. Nothing between that and
[[C4. Transform Evidence into CV Bullets]] ever chose a *set*, so section sizes tracked how much
evidence was approved rather than what a two-page CV can hold. This step is where that choice is
made: it decides what C4 is even asked to rewrite.

## A · What this step does

**It ranks; it does not approve.** The Keep gate in C2 is a human judgement about truthfulness and is
not being automated away. C3 writes `requirement_tailoring.shortlist_rank` — 1..B for the evidence it
chose, `NULL` for the rest — and the owner overrides it in either direction (§D). Nothing here can
put on the CV something the owner did not first Keep.

**Its unit is a distinct evidence ref, not a row.** One ref becomes one bullet. `requirement_tailoring`
holds one row per requirement×evidence link, and the same strong bullet legitimately answers several
requirements — 64 Kept rows on one measured lead were 35 distinct refs. Reasoning about the budget in
rows sets it about a third too tight.

**It runs in code, not as a model call.** The objective in §B is submodular under a cardinality
constraint, so greedy plus a pairwise swap pass is effectively exact at these sizes and runs in
milliseconds. It is also deterministic and explainable — the step report says why each bullet was
chosen and what it displaced, which a model call cannot. House rule, from the head of
`lib/pipeline/tailoring.ts`: the LLM emits judgments, code decides. Selection is arithmetic over
judgments C2 already made.

## B · The objective

```
V(S) = Σ_q  w(q) × max{ s(q,e) : e ∈ S, e links q }     coverage, quality-weighted
     + α × |distinct requirement_skills covered by S|    ATS keyword breadth
     + β × Σ_{e∈S} impact(e)                             quantified-outcome bonus

  w(q)   = 3 Core | 2 Important | 1 Nice-to-Have
  s(q,e) = 1.0 Excellent | 0.85 Very Strong | 0.7 Good | 0.4 Weak
  maximise V(S)  subject to  |S| ≤ B  and the §C constraints
```

**The `max` is the mechanism.** A second bullet on a requirement already covered at Excellent adds
almost nothing, so redundancy stops paying and breadth wins without any explicit anti-duplication
rule. That is also what makes V submodular.

`impact(e)` reads a measurable outcome off the evidence: full credit where the node carries its own
`metric` column (STAR results — those figures appear nowhere in the prose), half credit for a
percentage, currency amount or magnitude in the text itself. A bare integer is scale, not
measurement, and does not count.

## C · Constraints that make it a CV and not a score

- **Per-position cap** (default 4). Cardinality alone will happily put nine bullets on one role and
  none on the next.
- **A floor of ≥1 on the most recent roles** (default the most recent three that have any candidate).
  Repaired explicitly after the greedy pass, paid for out of the most over-represented position, and
  recorded in the step report — "the algorithm put nothing under your current job" must never happen
  quietly.
- **Education and Language refs never enter the budget.** They render from the profile tables
  regardless of any of this, so they were never competing for CV space. This is also what stops a
  degree printing as a *skill*, at source: not selected → C4 writes no bullet → no `cv_bullet_skills`
  → nothing for C5 to print. Their own sections still show their detail only for rows the owner Kept.
- **`B` is a parameter, not a constant.** Default 14, from the owner's 13–16 estimate. See §E.

## D · The owner overrides it

`requirement_tailoring.shortlist_pin` carries the override, per evidence ref:

- **`pin`** — this evidence enters the set before the algorithm runs. It consumes budget, so
  something else falls out; the step report shows what.
- **`exclude`** — never selected.
- **`NULL`** — let C3 decide. The ordinary case.

A pin also overrides the per-position cap. The cap exists to stop the algorithm lopsiding the CV, not
to overrule the person.

## E · The budget is provisional, and must not pretend otherwise

CV template and output format are deliberately deferred until bullets and skills are right. A page
budget calibrated against a template that is about to change is a number nobody should trust —
`SKILLS_ENVELOPE = 40` is what that mistake looks like a month later. So `B` is a named, documented
parameter; the step report records the resulting bullet count and the objective value; and
re-calibration happens in the format CI, by changing the parameter, without touching the algorithm.

**Measured 2026-08-25, and this is the number that needs watching: the objective goes flat after
about the sixth pick.** 100% Core and Important coverage is reached in 4 bullets on one measured
lead, 5 on another, 6 on the third. Past that every remaining candidate has a marginal gain of zero,
so the back half of a 14-bullet budget is filled by tie-break (strongest standalone evidence) rather
than by the objective. The step report says how many bullets were "filled past saturation". A budget
the objective cannot spend is a fact about the budget, and the honest responses are re-calibration or
better recall upstream — not a cleverer tie-break.

## F · What it hands on

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `shortlist_rank` | 1..B in the order the selected evidence stands. `NULL` = not selected. |
| `shortlist_pin` | The owner's override: `pin` \| `exclude` \| `NULL`. |

C4 rewrites only the selected rows. C5 draws its skills from their `cv_bullet_skills`. C7 and C8 see
only what was selected, plus the Education and Language sections, which print regardless.

Rows that lose their place have their `cv_bullet` and `cv_bullet_skills` cleared in the same pass —
a stale bullet from a previous run would otherwise reach C5's Skills section and the Map's proof
trail as though it were on the CV.

## G · No system prompt

This step makes no model call, so it has no entry in `STEP_NOTE` (`lib/prompts.ts`) and this note is
never loaded as a system prompt. An empty note loaded as one would be worse than no entry at all.
