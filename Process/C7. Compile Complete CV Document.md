### Purpose  

Compile all outputs from previous steps (C2, C4, C5, and C6) into a clean, professional, final CV tailored to a specific Job Lead. The final CV must remain within **2 pages maximum** and follow strict space management and prioritization rules to ensure conciseness and relevance.

---

### A. Inputs

- Final cleaned **C2 export** for the Job Lead (particularly the “CV Position” column)
- CV Bullets produced in [[C4. Transform Evidence into CV Bullets]] 
- Profile text drafted in [[C6. Drafting CV Profile (Per Job Lead)]]
- Job Lead details (Job Title, City/Office, reference codes if available)
- Working  [[CV_Template.docx]]

---

### B. Execution Sequence

1. **Load the Working Template**  
   Start with the current approved CV Template.

2. **Apply Space Management & Prioritization Rules**
   - Profile and Skills arrive already within their budgets — C6 §B.6 and C5 §B.1 own those, and C7 does not restate them
   - Check the assembled document against the **2-page** limit, and trim per §C if it does not fit
   - Project labels remain visible in the final CV — for the projects that have bullets. A project with none loses its label with them

3. **Replace Placeholders**
   - Use the **"CV Position"** column from the final C2 export to map each CV Bullet to its correct location in the template.
   - Completely replace all `<< >>` placeholders with the actual CV Bullets.
   - No `<< >>` placeholders should remain visible in the final CV.

4. **Compile Each Section**

| Section                     | Source                          | Rules |
|----------------------------|----------------------------------|-------|
| **Header + Job Title**     | Job Lead details                | Include Job Title and reference code (if available) |
| **Personal Info**          | Template + Job Lead City        | Add “(Willing to relocate to [City])” if the Job Lead office is not Vienna |
| **Profile**                | C6                              | As C6 §B.6 produced it. Apply bold inline only when natural |
| **Skills**                 | C5                              | As C5 §B.1 produced it — categories, order and count are C5's |
| **Professional Experience**| C4 + C5 bullets                 | Map using "CV Position". A slot with no selected evidence renders nothing, caption included |
| **Education**              | Fixed                           | Keep shortened version |
| **Executive Education**    | Fixed                           | Keep as-is |
| **Languages**              | Fixed                           | Small separate section at the bottom |

5. **Headshot Handling**
   - If a headshot is to be included: Insert according to rules defined in C1.
   - If no headshot is included: Insert the following text:  
     *"Headshot not added in respect to D&I best practices."*

6. **Final Quality Check**
   - Confirm that no `<< >>` placeholders remain.
   - Verify the CV does not exceed **2 pages** — with `scripts/cv-pages.ps1`, which asks Word itself. An asserted page count is not a verified one, and that is how this rule went unimplemented for months while the document ran to three.
   - Check that all Core and Important skills are represented at least once.
   - Ensure consistent formatting and professional appearance.

7. **Output**
   - Final clean CV in Word format (PDF optional).
   - File naming should follow the convention defined in C1 (if applicable).

### C. Space Management & Prioritization Rules

**C7 is the compiler. It holds the page limit and nothing else.**

Each section's budget belongs to the step that produces the section — the Skills numbers to [[C5. Build and Manage the Skills Section]] §B.1, the Profile length to [[C6. Drafting CV Profile (Per Job Lead)]] §B.6 — and every one of them lives in `lib/cv-budget.ts`, cited rather than restated. The owner's framing, 2026-08-27: *"leaving C7 as much as possible as a simple compiler and orchestrator of the 2 page CV."*

That is a design decision and not a tidy-up. Two prompts could disagree about the Skills section only because both claimed authority over it; after this, one does.

| Rule                           | Limit                                      | Notes |
|--------------------------------|--------------------------------------------|-------|
| **Maximum Pages**              | Strictly **2 pages**                       | Non-negotiable. The one rule only C7 can enforce, because only C7 sees the assembled document |
| **What gives way**             | The **lowest-ranked project bullet**, from the end of C3's own `shortlist_rank` order | Last resort, and reported on the C7 step. Never a role overview, and never a position's last surviving bullet |
| **Role Overview Label**        | Keep **"Role Overview:"**                  | As per current decision |
| **Languages**                  | Small separate section at the bottom, **on one line** | Fixed content. As four bulleted lines this section was the entire page-3 overflow on three of five measured leads |

**How the page limit is checked without opening Word.** The renderer estimates the line cost of everything the upstream steps decide — profile, skills, bullets, captions — at the template's measured column width, and compares it with `CONTENT_LINE_ALLOWANCE`. The template's fixed furniture is not modelled but absorbed into that allowance, which is therefore **calibrated against Word rather than derived**: `scripts/measure-cv-space.ts` prints the estimate, `scripts/cv-pages.ps1` prints Word's verdict, and the allowance is right while every lead the first passes the second calls two pages. Re-calibrate whenever the template's fixed paragraphs change.

**An empty slot renders nothing.** A project with no selected evidence loses its bullets *and its caption*, and the surviving projects renumber; a position with no selected role overview loses that paragraph. Nothing is topped up from the bullet bank — see §D.

*Superseded (2026-08-27, [[C7 Space Rules Are Specified and Never Enforced]]) — three rows left this table:*

- ***Profile, maximum 5 lines.** Moved to C6 §B.6, where it is now six lines expressed as a word count, because a line count is not something a model that cannot see the rendering is able to obey.*
- ***Skills Section, max 4 categories × 5.** Moved to C5 §B.1 as a target of 4 × 5 under a ceiling of 5 × 6. C5 §B.1 had said 3–5 × 4–8 the whole time, and C5 is the note loaded as a system prompt while this one is not — so this table was the version a reader would quote, and C5's was the version the model was actually given.*
- ***Bullet Inclusion, only bullets linked to Core or Important skills.** **Retired as redundant, measured rather than assumed.** [[C3. Select the CV Evidence Set]]'s objective already weights Core 3 / Important 2 / Nice-to-Have 1, and a coverage-maximising selection does not spend a slot on evidence answering only Nice-to-Have requirements while Core ones are still uncovered. Checked across the whole catalogue on 2026-08-27: **0 of 82 selected bullets, over 174 leads, would have been dropped by this filter.** Building it would have added a second mechanism that agrees with the objective until the day it does not.*

### D. Notes & Quality Rules

- The final CV must look like a **professional, finished document**, not a working template.
- All placeholders (`<< >>`) must be fully replaced before output.
- The CV should feel focused and relevant to the specific Job Lead.
- Education and Executive Education remain fixed and shortened.
- Languages remain as a small, separate section at the bottom.
- This step is the final compilation before the CV is ready for submission.
- Project labels remain visible in the final CV (as per latest decision) — for the projects that print. A project the Keep set did not reach loses its label along with its bullets, and the projects that remain renumber from 1.
- **Nothing is refilled.** Until 2026-08-27 a slot the Keep set did not cover was topped up from the bullet bank (projects) or the position's responsibilities (role overviews), so that "the real 2-page template never renders a blank section". That is why the CV's length had stopped depending on how much evidence was selected: sweeping [[C3. Select the CV Evidence Set]]'s budget from 14 to 9 moved the page count not at all. Worse than neutral, in fact — the refill is per-slot and unbounded, so a slot losing its one tailored bullet came back with up to four bank ones and the line count went *up* as bullets came out. Across five measured leads the refill was contributing 2 / 4 / 6 / 10 / 12 lines nobody had selected.
